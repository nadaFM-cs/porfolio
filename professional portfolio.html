Kinetix — AI-to-CNC design pipeline

Led the development of Kinetix, a CNC-integrated mobile application that bridges design and fabrication through AI. Users draw freehand, upload an image, or capture one with the camera, and send it to an AI model over HTTP — the AI processes the image and sends it on to a CNC machine, which brings the design to life.

Also built a prompt-based image generation feature: the AI generates an image from text, and the CNC draws it automatically. The backend runs on FastAPI, keeping the full pipeline seamless and efficient.

InputDraw, upload, camera, or a text prompt
HTTPFlutter sends it to the FastAPI backend
AIModel processes or generates the image
CNCMachine draws the final design
